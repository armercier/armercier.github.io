##### Summary

Commercial borehole seismic instruments are expensive and closed, which limits who can collect vertical seismic profiling data. This paper presents an open reference design for a multi-component borehole seismic array that can be deployed on a standard mineral-industry wireline winch. It documents the acquisition electronics, firmware and control software, reports the prototype's performance in bench tests, and describes a first field validation.
