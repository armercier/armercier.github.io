##### Summary

Active seismic data have known sources and even coverage, while passive events cover a larger volume but cluster along faults and come with unknown source parameters. This preprint combines both in one tomographic inversion, using an optimised (QR-based) selection of passive events so that the two data types can be treated consistently without ad hoc weighting, and so that densely sampled regions do not dominate. Applied to active and passive data from the BedrettoLab rock laboratory in Ticino, Switzerland, the combined model illuminates about three times the volume of the active-only model while retaining the information of the full data set. Residuals also point to anisotropy linked to the largely parallel fracture network.
