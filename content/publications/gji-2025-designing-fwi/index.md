##### Summary

The computational cost of 3-D multiparameter full-waveform inversion comes from both the number of experiments and the number of model parameters. This paper tackles the two together: it designs the source layout and a wavelet-based model parametrisation in one combined criterion. In synthetic acoustic studies the approach roughly halves the number of sources and compresses the model by about 90%, which makes fast Gauss–Newton inversion practical. The paper also proposes a post-acquisition strategy for selecting source–receiver pairs, to extract compact datasets from large surveys and reduce the number of simulations.
