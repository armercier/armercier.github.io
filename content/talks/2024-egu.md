##### About the talk

Optimising both which source–receiver pairs are used and how the subsurface model is parameterised reduces the cost of FWI. In early synthetic 2-D acoustic tests, the number of source–receiver pairs was roughly halved and model parameters cut by about 90% while keeping most of the information content. This work led to the [GJI paper](/publications/gji-2025-designing-fwi/).
